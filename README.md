# 🩸 Aarogyam — Blood & Organ Donor Network

> **Connecting Life, One Match at a Time.**

Aarogyam is a real-time, city-based emergency allocation platform connecting individual donors, recipients, and hospitals for transparent blood and posthumous organ matching.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- 🩸 **Emergency Blood Matching**: Rapid algorithmic matching connecting hospital emergency requests with eligible local donors.
- 🫀 **Posthumous Organ Pledge Registry**: Secure digital pledge registry enabling individuals to register consent for post-mortem organ donation with downloadable donor cards and QR verification.
- ⚡ **Explainable AI Matching Engine**: Multi-factor scoring algorithm evaluating blood compatibility (50%), geographic proximity (30%), and donor reliability (20%).
- 🚚 **Simulated Cold-Chain Logistics**: Real-time dispatch workflows with temperature monitoring, transit telemetry, and route tracking.
- 🔒 **Cryptographic Transparency Ledger**: Immutable SHA-256 allocation hashing to ensure auditability, fairness, and zero favoritism.
- 🏆 **Gamified Donor Engagement**: Lifesaver tier progression (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum), achievement badges, and community engagement to solve donor drop-off.
- 🏥 **Hospital Command Center**: Dedicated portal for medical centers to create urgent requests, view matched candidates, and monitor live dispatches.
- 🗺️ **City Hospital & Donor Network**: Interactive geographic mapping of verified healthcare institutions and donor coverage zones.
- 📊 **Predictive AI Insights & Analytics**: Clinical demand forecasting, city-level inventory recommendations, and allocation trend analysis.
